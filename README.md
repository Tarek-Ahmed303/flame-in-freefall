# 🔥 Flame in Freefall

### AI-Powered Fire Safety Insights from Microgravity Combustion Data

🚀 **NASA Space Apps Challenge 2026**

---

## 🌌 About the Challenge

NASA has been studying how fire behaves in **microgravity** for decades.

Fire does not behave exactly the same way in space as it does on Earth. NASA has collected a large amount of information from these experiments, but finding, understanding, and comparing all of this information can be difficult.

With future missions to the Moon and Mars, understanding fire behavior in space is an important part of keeping astronauts and spacecraft safe.

---

## 💡 Our Idea

**Flame in Freefall** is an interactive AI-powered platform that makes NASA's microgravity combustion research easier to explore.

Users will be able to ask questions about NASA's experiments and get clear answers based on the available scientific information.

For example:

> 🔎 "How does oxygen concentration affect flame behavior in microgravity?"

The platform will help users:

- 🔥 Explore fire experiments in space
- 🔎 Search through NASA's research
- 📊 Compare different experiments
- 🤖 Ask questions using AI
- 📚 Understand important findings
- 🛡️ Discover potential fire-safety insights

---

## 🎯 Our Goal

Our goal is to turn a large amount of complex scientific information into something that is:

**Easy to explore.  
Easy to understand.  
Useful for discovering insights.**

We want to connect decades of NASA research with modern AI to help people better understand how fire behaves beyond Earth.

---

## 🖥️ What We Are Building

The project will provide an interactive experience where users can:

### 🔎 Search

Find NASA experiments related to a specific topic or question.

### 📊 Compare

Look at different experiments and understand how their conditions and results differ.

### 🤖 Ask AI

Ask questions about the research and receive answers based on the available NASA information.

### 🛡️ Explore Safety Insights

Highlight findings that could be relevant to fire safety during future human space missions.
